Corner Company
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Provided by NewMeans

Your keyboard activity and AI conversations stay on your Mac. Version 1.2.1 reads input counts and pointer movement locally without requesting Accessibility or Input Monitoring. It does not learn which keys you type or upload text typed in other apps or AI conversations. Entering Rankings with Game Center and iCloud available publishes your equipped appearance and item-derived score under your Game Center display name. The core companion works without opening Rankings. Earlier beta versions are described separately below.

1. Keyboard and mouse activity

Version 1.2.1 reads macOS event counters for key presses, releases, modifier activity, and mouse clicks, the number of held modifier keys, and pointer position. It uses these locally for animations, typing sounds, activity totals, pointer-distance estimates, and in-app progress, including while you use other apps. It requests neither Accessibility nor Input Monitoring.

The counters do not identify the keys or mouse buttons you used. The pet chooses its own keys for animation. Corner Company does not receive text typed in other apps or save a keystroke history; only aggregate activity statistics are saved locally. Input behavior in Secure Input contexts can vary by macOS version and remains part of beta testing.

Earlier beta builds used physical key events in memory with macOS input permissions. They did not save a keystroke history or reconstruct and save text typed in other apps.

2. Information saved on your Mac

Corner Company saves your progress and preferences locally, including activity totals, virtual currency, collections, equipped items, timers, language, window position, and any task text or preset names you choose to enter into Corner Company. This intentionally entered text is separate from keyboard activity observed in other apps.

When you export a pet photo, the app writes a PNG to the location you choose. This feature captures the app's pet scene, not your desktop or the contents of other apps. The app does not provide cloud synchronization of game progress. Optional public ranking entries are described in section 5.

3. Local AI activity integration

To indicate whether a supported AI coding tool is working, the app can read local Codex and Claude Code session files that it is allowed to access. These files may contain prompts, responses, and tool output. Corner Company processes records locally to identify session activity, timestamps, turn boundaries, and control markers; it does not retain a separate copy of the conversations or transmit them to NewMeans or an AI provider.

In the TestFlight/Mac App Store build, AI folders start disconnected. You choose a session folder through the macOS folder picker. The app saves the selected path and a macOS security-scoped bookmark locally so that it can restore that connection. Choosing Disconnect stops monitoring and removes the saved connection; resetting the app also clears these connections.

The original session files belong to the corresponding AI tool. Resetting Corner Company does not delete those files. Local diagnostic errors may include file paths and error information; Corner Company does not automatically upload these diagnostics.

4. Support and TestFlight

If you contact newmeans.dev@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the reported issue. Please remove private text and unrelated files before sending a screenshot or diagnostic report. Our email provider processes support correspondence to deliver and store email.

If you use the TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash and usage information and shares it with us. Apple also shares feedback you submit. Depending on the invitation method, this may include your name and email address, as well as app and OS versions and device information. We use TestFlight data to test and improve Corner Company, not for advertising, and do not share it with third parties. See Apple's TestFlight & Privacy notice.

5. Optional public rankings

Version 1.2.1: Game Center names and CloudKit appearances

Opening Lounge → Rankings connects to Apple's Game Center. Once Game Center and the Mac's existing iCloud account are available, the app automatically publishes your equipped keyboard, pet, and accessory appearance. Later committed equipment changes update the same public profile during that app session. Startup, typing, and opening My stats alone do not activate publication.

Game Center processes the player identity and display name, item-derived score, rank, and a reference to the appearance profile. CloudKit's public database stores catalog identifiers and attributes of the equipped appearance, with Apple-managed record information. Other players can read the public profile, and ranking views show the Game Center display name. Version 1.2.1 has no app nickname field and sends no app nickname in its appearance document. It offers only the app's catalog choices, with no custom text or image upload in Rankings. Preview pictures are rendered locally. Input counts, keystrokes, task text, preset names, inventory, and AI conversations are not part of these public records.

Apple handles sign-in through its system services. Corner Company does not receive your Apple Account password or store a Game Center authentication credential. Game Center sign-in is required for Rankings; public appearance reads do not require iCloud sign-in, while publication waits for iCloud availability. A Game Center/iCloud account pair identifies the profile. The app keeps the latest pending appearance locally for its Game Center player and retries failed changes. A player-scoped local cache can retain public ranking entries and their profiles for up to seven days for offline viewing.

There is no in-app Remove control. Local reset cancels pending changes and deactivates publication until Rankings is entered again. It does not delete published Game Center scores or CloudKit profiles, and a request already accepted by Apple may still finish. Uninstalling also does not delete these records. Contact NewMeans for requests about public appearance information we manage; Apple's account and Game Center privacy controls remain available in system settings. See Game Center & Privacy and iCloud & Privacy for Apple's handling of these services. Versions 1.2.0 and 1.2.1 do not call the older NewMeans ranking API.

Version 1.2.0: earlier CloudKit profiles with app nicknames

Version 1.2.0 also automatically published the app nickname when Rankings was opened and updated it after nickname edits. That nickname was a separate app label, not the Game Center name. Existing CloudKit records may retain this older field until a later appearance save replaces the document. Version 1.2.1 ignores the older field and uses the Game Center display name. Upgrading alone does not delete earlier public records; the account, caching, reset, and removal limitations above also apply to 1.2.0.

Version 1.1.0: the earlier NewMeans ranking service

The following describes entries shared by the 1.1.0 beta. They are not imported into Game Center or removed by upgrading to 1.2.0 or 1.2.1.

Opening Rankings contacts a NewMeans API hosted on Google Cloud to retrieve public entries. You can browse without an account or an installation key. Entering a local nickname does not publish it. Choosing Share my look sends your nickname and the catalog identifiers and attributes of the appearance you chose to share. The service stores the entry, its item-based score, and publication/update timestamps, and makes the nickname and appearance available to other users. Preview pictures are rendered locally; this feature does not upload a screenshot or photo.

On your first explicit publication, the app creates a random installation identifier and secret to manage that installation's entry. It encrypts them locally using the operating system's secure storage. The server stores a cryptographic hash of the credential to check ownership; the secret is not included in public entries. This is separate from an email/password account and does not provide cross-device account recovery. Game activity counts, keystrokes, tasks, preset names, and AI conversation content are not part of ranking entries.

Changing equipment does not automatically replace your published look. Use Use current look to publish a new selection. If you already participate, editing your profile nickname updates the nickname on your existing entry. Queued publication changes and removal requests may retry when the connection returns or after relaunch.

The service uses Google Cloud Run and Firestore in the Seoul region. Google Cloud processes connection and request information, such as IP addresses, request times, and response metadata, to operate and secure the service. NewMeans uses short-lived IP-derived hashes for registration limits; their expiry is set to three days after the limit window, with deletion subject to asynchronous cleanup. Installation credential hashes, request ordering, and revocation records are stored separately from public entries to enforce ownership and prevent delayed requests from restoring removed data.

You can remove your public entry from My look. Removing an entry retains its installation credential for a later explicit rejoin. Resetting the app queues withdrawal of a shared entry; the request needs a working connection to finish. If you plan to uninstall, remove your entry while online first. Uninstalling alone does not send a removal request. Contact us about data held by NewMeans; do not send your installation secret.

6. Network services and this website

The current Corner Company app does not include advertising or a third-party analytics SDK. Apart from the optional ranking operations described above, it does not automatically send locally processed activity or saved game progress to NewMeans. Opening an external link, sending email, and installing or testing through Apple services are subject to those services' privacy practices.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process connection information such as your IP address and requested page to serve and secure the website. This page does not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Retention and your choices

  • Quitting the app stops its local activity processing. Version 1.2.1 needs no input privacy permission. Permissions previously granted to older builds can be managed in macOS System Settings.
  • Use the in-app reset control to delete Corner Company's saved progress, settings, and tasks. The app may retain your selected interface language. Exported photos, system diagnostics, backups, and other apps' session files are not removed by that reset.
  • App removal does not necessarily remove all local data. You control retained app files, exported photos, and backups on your Mac.
  • We retain support correspondence and any copies of beta reports only as long as reasonably needed to resolve requests, investigate issues, and improve the beta, unless a longer period is required by law. We delete or anonymize them when no longer needed. Apple controls retention within its TestFlight service.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern. We may need information to identify the relevant ranking entry, correspondence, or beta report. We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your Mac. Service providers such as Google Cloud, Apple, our email provider, and GitHub may process data in countries other than your own under their privacy policies.
