1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information only for the following purposes, and if a purpose changes, it takes the measures required by law.

  1. 1. Confirming purchases of paid items, delivering items and restoring purchases
  2. 2. Cancellations, refunds, refunds of overpayments and handling related disputes
  3. 3. Detecting and preventing fraudulent payments and abnormal use
  4. 4. Providing ads and ad rewards, measuring ad performance and preventing invalid ad traffic
  5. 5. Handling user inquiries and complaints
  6. 6. Fulfilling legal obligations

c. Information stored only on the device and not collected by the Company

Game progress (stages, currency, cards and items, missions and check-in records), settings (sound, vibration, notifications) and records of purchases already delivered are stored only on the user's device and are not sent to the Company or third parties. Notifications are scheduled and shown within the device, and the Company does not collect device tokens for sending notifications.

If the device operating system's backup feature (Android Auto Backup, iCloud Backup and so on) is turned on, this information may be backed up to the user's own cloud account. This is a feature of the operating system, and the Company does not access these backups.

3. Personal Information of Children under 14

The Game is not mainly intended for children under 14, and the Company does not collect children's personal information in ways that require a legal guardian's consent. If the Company learns that personal information of a child under 14 has been processed, it destroys it without delay. Legal guardians may request access to, deletion of, or suspension of processing of a child's personal information as described in "12. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them".

5. Procedure and Method of Destruction

  1. 1. Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay. If it must be kept under other laws, it is moved and stored separately and used only for that purpose.
  2. 2. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.
  3. 3. Purchase history kept in the payment tool is deleted when its retention period ends, at the Company's request to the processor.
  4. 4. Game progress stored on the device is deleted when the user deletes the app or clears its data.

6. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. The exceptions are where a law specifically provides for it, or where an investigative agency requests it for an investigation in accordance with the procedures and methods set by law.

9. Measures to Keep Personal Information Secure

The Company takes the following measures to keep personal information secure.

  1. 1. Administrative measures: keeping the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum, and managing and supervising processors.
  2. 2. Technical measures: encrypting (TLS) the connections over which personal information is sent, using 2-step verification for the payment management tool and the store console accounts, and giving access rights only to those who need them.
  3. 3. Physical measures: controlling access to devices and documents that contain personal information.

10. Installation, Operation and Refusal of Automatic Collection Tools

The Game does not use cookies. The device's advertising identifier may be collected automatically to provide ads, and users can block or change it as follows.

DeviceHow
AndroidSettings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID
iPhoneSettings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or turn off tracking for the Game in the list

The Game can be used even if the advertising identifier is blocked. Ads may still appear, but they are general ads rather than ads tailored to interests.

If you do not want personalized ads, you can do the following.

  2. 2. Turn off personalized ads in Google My Ad Center (https://myadcenter.google.com).
  3. 3. In regions where a consent screen is provided, change your choice in the Game under Settings (설정) > Ad Privacy (광고 개인정보).

The Company does not collect behavioral information to provide personalized ads to children it knows to be under 14, and does not collect sensitive behavioral information such as ideology, beliefs or health.

12. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

  1. 1. Users (or legal guardians, for children under 14) may at any time request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of their personal information, and withdraw consent.
  2. 2. Requests can be made at newmeans.dev@gmail.com, and the Company acts on them and reports the result within 10 days from the day it receives them.
  3. 3. Because the Game has no accounts, you must include a store order number (for example, a Google Play order number starting with GPA) or the user ID shown on the Game's settings screen so that your information can be found. The Company may check whether the person making the request is the data subject or a legitimate representative.
  4. 4. Personal information that other laws require to be kept cannot be deleted on request, and requests for access or suspension of processing may be restricted by law. In that case, the Company tells you the reason.
  5. 5. Requests made through a representative must include a power of attorney.

15. Use Outside the Republic of Korea

  1. 1. In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen about ads appears when the Game is first launched, and information is used for personalized ads only to the extent you consent. You can change your choice at any time under Settings (설정) > Ad Privacy (광고 개인정보).
  2. 2. On iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it on the screen that asks for permission to track.
  3. 3. The Company does not sell personal information.

16. Changes to This Policy

When this policy is changed, the Company gives notice on this page and on the notices page of the Company website (https://newmeans.dev/MagicalDove/en/notices, linked from the Game's settings screen) from 7 days before the change takes effect. Changes that significantly affect users' rights, such as adding items collected, are announced from 30 days before.
