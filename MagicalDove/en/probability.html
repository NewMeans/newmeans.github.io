Duplicates and levels

  • A joker drawn for the first time applies immediately at Lv 1. When a joker you already have is drawn, it is added to that joker's level material, and the joker levels up when the required number is reached (up to Lv 20). The effect at each level and the required number are shown in the game's joker window.
  • When a joker at its highest level is drawn again, Stardust 5 is returned.
  • When all jokers are at their highest level, the draw is not available.

2. Pet draw, prop draw

Pets and magic props are drawn under the same rules.

ItemDetails
Cost3 free a day, 2 more by watching ads, then Diamonds 200 for 1 draw
Result1 D-grade pet or prop (grade odds 100%)
Pet eggsA pet egg received from touring shows and check-in rewards counts as 1 pet draw, with the same odds
PityNone

The result is the same whether you draw for free, with an ad, with Diamonds or with a pet egg, and at any stage. Each grade has one pet and one prop, so the D-grade pet is the Rabbit in a Hat (모자 속 토끼) and the D-grade prop is the Silk Handkerchief (실크 손수건).

Higher grades are made by merging 2 of the same grade (merging always gives 1 of the next grade up; no odds involved).
