Duplicates and levels

  • A joker drawn for the first time applies immediately at Lv 1. When a joker you already have is drawn, it is added to that joker's level material, and the joker levels up when the required number is reached (up to Lv 20). The effect at each level and the required number are shown in the game's joker window.
  • When a joker at its highest level is drawn again, Stardust 1 is returned.
  • When all jokers are at their highest level, the draw is not available.

2. Pet draw, prop draw

Pets and magic props are drawn under the same rules.

ItemDetails
Cost3 free a day, 2 more by watching ads, then Diamonds 100 for 1 draw
Result1 D-grade pet or prop (grade odds 100%)
Pet eggsA pet egg received from touring shows and check-in rewards counts as 1 pet draw, with the same odds
PityNone

The result is the same whether you draw for free, with an ad, with Diamonds or with a pet egg, and at any stage. Each grade has one pet and one prop, so the D-grade pet is the Rabbit in a Hat (모자 속 토끼) and the D-grade prop is the Silk Handkerchief (실크 손수건).

Higher grades are made by merging 2 of the same grade (merging always gives 1 of the next grade up; no odds involved).

4. Card merge

ItemDetails
Materials2 cards of the same grade
Grade1 card of the next grade up (odds 100%, never fails). Grade order: D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS, X, XX, XXX, Z, ZZ, ZZZ
Suit and numberNewly determined regardless of the materials. One of the 52 kinds left after excluding those of the cards now equipped in the deck. Each kind has the weight below, and the odds of a kind are its weight ÷ the sum of the weights of all possible kinds

4.1 Weights

  • The base is 1.
  • When the merged card goes into the deck (into that card's place when merged into a card in the deck, or, when merged in storage, when the card would go in and make a stronger deck), a kind that brings the deck one card closer to a hand has the weight 1 + Strength × hand weight × 0.4^(the number of cards the deck is still short of that hand after the kind goes in).
    • Cards short: how many of the deck's cards would have to be different cards to make the hand (if the deck has fewer than 5 cards, the empty slots count as cards yet to come).
    • Hands that are still more than 2 cards short after it goes in are not counted.
    • If the kind completes a hand (0 cards short after it goes in), the value for hands from One Pair (원페어) to Full House (풀하우스) is multiplied by the completion multiplier (4.2). It is not applied to Four of a Kind (포카드), Straight Flush (스트레이트 플러시) or Royal Straight Flush (로얄 스트레이트 플러시).
    • If it comes closer to several hands, only the largest of those values is used.
  • If the deck has 5 cards and the merged card is lower than the lowest grade in the deck (when merging in storage), every kind has weight 1.
  • If every kind has weight 1, all possible kinds have the same odds (4.4 below).
