2. Cancellation (refunds)

  1. 1. Unused paid items can be refunded within 7 days from the day of purchase.
  2. 2. The following cannot be refunded.
    • Items applied or delivered immediately on purchase (marked "restricted" in the table above)
    • Diamonds and Stardust already used, and currency spent on draws
    • Items whose bonus benefits have already been used
  3. 3. If an item differs from what was shown or advertised, or was not delivered as purchased, it can be refunded within 3 months from the day it was received, or within 30 days from the day you learned of this.
  4. 4. When a refund is made, the currency and benefits received from that item are taken back.

3. How to request a refund

StoreHow
Google PlayWithin 48 hours of purchase, request it through Google Play's refund request (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). After that, request it at newmeans.dev@gmail.com with the order number (starting with GPA)

What to send when requesting a refund from the Company: the store order number or receipt, the item purchased and the date, and the reason for the refund. The Company starts the refund process within 3 business days from the day it finishes checking.

4. Payments by minors

If a minor pays without the consent of a legal guardian, the minor or the legal guardian can cancel the payment. It cannot be canceled if the payment was made with property within the scope the legal guardian allowed, or if the minor pretended to be an adult. When requesting, send documents proving the family relationship and material confirming the name on the payment method to newmeans.dev@gmail.com.

5. Game data and restoring purchases

  • Game progress and currency are stored only on the device. If you delete the app or change devices, progress, Diamonds, Stardust and items cannot be restored.
  • Remove Ads, Auto Pack, Special Lighting Pack and Show Plus are applied again when you tap Settings (설정) > Restore Purchases (구매 복원) with the same store account.
  • If you paid but did not receive the item, restart the game or tap Restore Purchases, and if it still does not arrive, write to newmeans.dev@gmail.com with the order number.

6. When the service ends

When the service ends, the Company gives notice at least 30 days before the end date and refunds paid items that are unused, or whose period of use remains, as of the end date. A refund contact (newmeans.dev@gmail.com) is kept open for at least 30 days from the end date.
