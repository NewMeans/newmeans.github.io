Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Typer app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by NewMeans (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application collects certain information automatically to operate the service, understand how it is used, improve gameplay, and measure advertising. We use Unity Gaming Services Analytics and Google Analytics for Firebase for analytics, and Google Mobile Ads and its mediation partners to provide and measure ads.

  1. Collected Information
    • Device or other IDs: App-instance and installation identifiers, and advertising identifiers when permitted by your device settings, platform permissions, and applicable consent choices.
    • App Activity: Session and app-open information, gameplay progress and results, stages, in-game interactions, settings actions, virtual item and currency activity, ad-related actions, purchase events, and widget usage counts. We do not receive your payment card details.
    • App and Device Information: App version, operating system, device model, language, and general geographic information such as country or region.
    • Advertising Information: Ad requests, impressions, interactions, availability, and related consent or privacy signals used to deliver and measure ads.
    • Advertising Revenue: For each ad impression, the ad format, estimated revenue and currency, and ad network name are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase and associated with app-instance analytics data.
    • Purchase-Linking Token: A value derived from the Firebase app-instance ID using SHA-256 is sent to Apple or Google when you make a purchase. We do not send the original app-instance ID in this payment field. The token allows us to connect store transaction records with the Application's analytics records for revenue measurement.
    • Payment and Refund Records: Information received from Apple and Google through server notifications and transaction queries, including the product, amount and currency, tax and net proceeds where available, country, purchase and refund times, store transaction or order identifiers, and the purchase-linking token. Google's original notifications also include a purchase token.
    • Store Sales Reports: Daily sales reports from App Store Connect and monthly sales reports from Play Console, including order numbers where available, country, and sales amounts.
  2. Purpose of Collection
    • To operate and improve the Application
    • To analyze user experience, gameplay patterns, and feature performance
    • To measure purchases, advertising, and product performance
    • To aggregate purchase and advertising revenue and measure lifetime value (LTV) and return on ad spend (ROAS) by acquisition campaign
    • To identify issues and optimize service quality

We do not use these additional revenue-measurement records to personalize advertising or to carry out refunds within the Application.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Yes. We use the following service providers to process information for the purposes described above:

  • Unity Technologies: Unity Gaming Services Analytics and advertising services. See Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC: Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, consent management, and Google Cloud services, including BigQuery and server processing for revenue measurement. See Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Advertising mediation partners: Google Mobile Ads may make ad requests available to mediation partners selected for the Application. Those partners process information under their own privacy policies and the consent choices presented in the Application.

Apple and Google also process in-app purchases under their own terms and privacy policies. The purchase-linking token is included in their payment records. We receive payment and refund notifications, transaction query results, and sales reports from those platforms for the revenue measurement described above. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Selected Google Analytics for Firebase event data, including advertising revenue events, is exported to our Google Cloud BigQuery project for internal analysis by NewMeans. We store these exports, our payment ledger, and downloaded sales reports in the Seoul region of South Korea. Access to exported analytics data is restricted to authorized personnel and service accounts; access to payment records and sales reports is limited to authorized administrators and the service accounts used by our processing functions.

International data processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is governed by their privacy policies and applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

The Seoul storage location applies to the copies held in our Google Cloud project. Google Analytics for Firebase and Apple and Google payment services may process the information they receive, including advertising revenue data, purchase-linking tokens, and transaction records, outside South Korea and your country of residence.

What are my opt-out rights?

Where privacy options are required or available, you can open the privacy control from the Application's Settings screen to review or change advertising consent choices for Google Mobile Ads and its mediation partners. Changing these advertising consent choices does not by itself disable analytics collection. You can also manage advertising tracking permissions in your device settings.

Uninstalling the Application stops future collection from the Application on that device. It does not by itself delete records already collected or stop Apple or Google from sending notifications about earlier purchases. To request access to or deletion of records held by NewMeans, contact us as described below.

Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Analytics and advertising data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to the retention controls and deletion processes available in Unity, Google Analytics, Google Cloud BigQuery, and our advertising services. We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed, unless a longer period is required by law.

  • Advertising revenue events: These follow the same retention periods as our other Google Analytics for Firebase event data. Retention and deletion of exported copies are managed separately in our BigQuery project.
  • Original payment and refund notifications: These are automatically deleted from our payment ledger one year after receipt.
  • Normalized transaction records, including records derived from store sales reports: These are retained for the duration of the service's operation to calculate LTV, subject to earlier deletion when they are no longer needed or as required by applicable law.
  • Tokens in store payment records: Apple and Google retain purchase-linking tokens in accordance with their own retention policies and legal obligations.

If applicable law requires us to retain particular transaction records, we retain the required records for the applicable statutory period.

The Application does not require account registration, so analytics and revenue records are generally associated with app-instance, installation, or device identifiers and purchase-linking tokens rather than a NewMeans account. If you wish to request access to or deletion of data associated with your device or purchases, contact us at newmeans.dev@gmail.com. We will review the request and may ask for information reasonably necessary to locate the relevant records. Our ability to identify particular records may be limited if the identifier has been reset or is not available.

Children

Parents and legal guardians should monitor their children's use of the Application. If you believe that a child has provided directly identifying information to the Service Provider, contact us at newmeans.dev@gmail.com so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect information the Service Provider processes and maintains.
