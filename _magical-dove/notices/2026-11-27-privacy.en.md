# Changes to the Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27

What changed: With the introduction of a marketing measurement tool (AppsFlyer) and Meta's ad measurement tool (Facebook SDK) in the Game for Android, the following was added.

- Purposes of processing: running ads that promote the Game, measuring installs and use that come from ads, and preventing fraudulent installs
- Items processed: marketing measurement (Android) items (player ID, advertising identifier, device ID created by the measurement tool, install source, device and app information, part of the Game usage records, purchase history, choices made on the consent screen), and the player ID among the analytics tools' items
- Retention period: marketing measurement records 24 months
- Entrustment: WEMADE PLAY Co., Ltd. (주식회사 위메이드플레이), for running ads and measuring their performance; AppsFlyer Ltd., for the marketing measurement tool (sub-entrustment)
- Transfer abroad: AppsFlyer Ltd. (Israel; stored in the European Union and the United States)
- Collection of behavioral information by ad providers: Meta Platforms, Inc. (United States), and how to refuse it

Why: To measure the performance of ads that promote the Game.
