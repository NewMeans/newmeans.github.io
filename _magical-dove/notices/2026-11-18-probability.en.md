# Changes to the Random Item Odds

Reference date: 2026-11-17 (document version 1.4)

What changed: The random item odds page was updated to reflect adjustments made before release. The differences from version 1.0, published earlier, are as follows.

- Card merge: each kind of suit and number now has a weight. Kinds that bring the deck closer to a hand come up more often. The weights and examples are in section 4 of the page.
- Dice (rerolling a deck card): a new section was added. It is section 5 of the page.
- Joker draw: the cost of 1 draw changed from Stardust 20 to 5, and the Stardust returned when a joker at its highest level is drawn again changed from 5 to 1. The effect values of the Nap, Applause, Shortcut and Teleport jokers changed. The odds by kind are unchanged.
- Pet draw, prop draw: the cost of 1 draw with Diamonds changed from 200 to 100. The odds are unchanged.
- The grade of Daily Card 3 changed from A to S.
