# Changes to the Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27

What changed: With the introduction of the ranking (Google Play Games Services) in the Game for Android, the following was added. The ranking comes with a later update of the Game for Android and is not in the version distributed now.

- Purposes of processing: providing the ranking (leaderboard)
- Items processed: Google Play Games player ID and display name, best stage record, rank. The Company does not store display names, player IDs or ranks, and only the display names and records of users who have set their profiles to public are visible to other users
- Retention and destruction: ranking records are kept until the user deletes this Game's data in their Google Play Games profile
- Entrustment and transfer abroad: Google LLC (United States), for storing ranking records and providing the leaderboard
- Automatic collection tools: the Google Play Games tool (Play Games Services SDK) checks the sign-in status when the Game is launched, and sends analytics data and diagnostic data to Google

Why: To provide the ranking.
