# Magical Dove Random Item Odds

The types and odds of items in the game whose results are determined by chance. Diamonds (다이아) and Stardust (별가루) are currencies that can be earned by playing the game or bought in the shop.

Reference date: 2026-11-13 (version 1.0.0)

## 1. Joker draw

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cost | Stardust 20 for 1 draw |
| Result | 1 of 16 jokers |
| Pity, odds changes | None. The odds are the same regardless of the number of draws or the jokers owned |

### Odds by grade

| Grade | Odds | Number of jokers |
|---|---|---|
| Common (일반) | 60% | 6 |
| Rare (희귀) | 28% | 5 |
| Epic (영웅) | 10% | 3 |
| Legendary (전설) | 2% | 2 |

### Duplicates and levels

- A joker drawn for the first time applies immediately at Lv 1. When a joker you already have is drawn, it is added to that joker's level material, and the joker levels up when the required number is reached (up to Lv 20). The effect at each level and the required number are shown in the game's joker window.
- When a joker at its highest level is drawn again, Stardust 5 is returned.
- When all jokers are at their highest level, the draw is not available.

## 2. Pet draw, prop draw

Pets and magic props are drawn under the same rules.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cost | 3 free a day, 2 more by watching ads, then Diamonds 200 for 1 draw |
| Result | 1 D-grade pet or prop (grade odds 100%) |
| Pet eggs | A pet egg received from touring shows and check-in rewards counts as 1 pet draw, with the same odds |
| Pity | None |

The result is the same whether you draw for free, with an ad, with Diamonds or with a pet egg, and at any stage. Each grade has one pet and one prop, so the D-grade pet is the Rabbit in a Hat (모자 속 토끼) and the D-grade prop is the Silk Handkerchief (실크 손수건).

Higher grades are made by merging 2 of the same grade (merging always gives 1 of the next grade up; no odds involved).

## 3. Card draw

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cost | Diamonds 100 for 1 draw |
| Result | 1 D-grade card (grade odds 100%) |
| Suit and number | 4 suits (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs) × 13 numbers (A, 2 to 10, J, Q, K) = 52 kinds at equal odds. 1 in 52 (1.9231%) for each kind |
| Pity, odds changes | None |

## 4. Card merge

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Materials | 2 cards of the same grade |
| Grade | 1 card of the next grade up (odds 100%, never fails). Grade order: D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS, X, XX, XXX, Z, ZZ, ZZZ |
| Suit and number | Newly determined regardless of the materials. Of the 52 kinds, those left after excluding the cards now equipped in the deck have equal odds |

Odds of each kind by the number of cards equipped in the deck (when merging into a card in the deck so that the result takes its place, that card is not counted):

| Cards equipped in the deck | Possible kinds | Odds of each kind |
|---|---|---|
| 0 cards | 52 | 1 in 52 (1.9231%) |
| 1 card | 51 | 1 in 51 (1.9608%) |
| 2 cards | 50 | 1 in 50 (2%) |
| 3 cards | 49 | 1 in 49 (2.0408%) |
| 4 cards | 48 | 1 in 48 (2.0833%) |
| 5 cards | 47 | 1 in 47 (2.1277%) |

## 5. Cards from items and rewards

The grade is fixed, and the suit and number are one of 52 kinds at equal odds (1 in 52 for each kind, 1.9231%).

| Source | Grade |
|---|---|
| Daily Card 1, 2, 3 | 1 card each of C, B and A |
| Step-up Card Pack | 3 cards. C on the 1st purchase, B on the 2nd, A on the 3rd, S on the 4th, SS from the 5th |
| Free draw (ad) | 1 D |
| Check-in day 5 | 1 B |
| Pass cards | A grade set by tier and season. At tiers 14, 17, 20 and 23, you pick 1 of 3 cards shown in advance |
