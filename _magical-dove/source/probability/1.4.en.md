# Magical Dove Random Item Odds

The types and odds of items in the game whose results are determined by chance. Diamonds (다이아) and Stardust (별가루) are currencies that can be earned by playing the game or bought in the shop.

Reference date: 2026-11-17 (version 1.0.0, QA branch values)

## 1. Joker draw

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cost | Stardust 5 for 1 draw |
| Result | 1 of 16 jokers |
| Pity, odds changes | None. The odds are the same regardless of the number of draws or the jokers owned |

| Common (일반) | 60% | 6 |
| Rare (희귀) | 28% | 5 |
| Epic (영웅) | 10% | 3 |

### Duplicates and levels

- A joker drawn for the first time applies immediately at Lv 1. When a joker you already have is drawn, it is added to that joker's level material, and the joker levels up when the required number is reached (up to Lv 20). The effect at each level and the required number are shown in the game's joker window.
- When a joker at its highest level is drawn again, Stardust 1 is returned.
- When all jokers are at their highest level, the draw is not available.

## 2. Pet draw, prop draw

Pets and magic props are drawn under the same rules.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cost | 3 free a day, 2 more by watching ads, then Diamonds 100 for 1 draw |
| Result | 1 D-grade pet or prop (grade odds 100%) |
| Pet eggs | A pet egg received from touring shows and check-in rewards counts as 1 pet draw, with the same odds |
| Pity | None |

The result is the same whether you draw for free, with an ad, with Diamonds or with a pet egg, and at any stage. Each grade has one pet and one prop, so the D-grade pet is the Rabbit in a Hat (모자 속 토끼) and the D-grade prop is the Silk Handkerchief (실크 손수건).

Higher grades are made by merging 2 of the same grade (merging always gives 1 of the next grade up; no odds involved).

## 3. Card draw

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Cost | Diamonds 100 for 1 draw |
| Result | 1 D-grade card (grade odds 100%) |
| Suit and number | 4 suits (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs) × 13 numbers (A, 2 to 10, J, Q, K) = 52 kinds at equal odds. 1 in 52 (1.9231%) for each kind |
| Pity, odds changes | None |

## 4. Card merge

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Materials | 2 cards of the same grade |
| Grade | 1 card of the next grade up (odds 100%, never fails). Grade order: D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS, X, XX, XXX, Z, ZZ, ZZZ |
| Suit and number | Newly determined regardless of the materials. One of the 52 kinds left after excluding those of the cards now equipped in the deck. Each kind has the weight below, and the odds of a kind are its weight ÷ the sum of the weights of all possible kinds |

### 4.1 Weights

- The base is 1.
- When the merged card goes into the deck (into that card's place when merged into a card in the deck, or, when merged in storage, when the card would go in and make a stronger deck), a kind that brings the deck one card closer to a hand has the weight **1 + Strength × hand weight × 0.4^(the number of cards the deck is still short of that hand after the kind goes in)**.
  - Cards short: how many of the deck's cards would have to be different cards to make the hand (if the deck has fewer than 5 cards, the empty slots count as cards yet to come).
  - Hands that are still more than 2 cards short after it goes in are not counted.
  - If the kind **completes** a hand (0 cards short after it goes in), the value for hands from One Pair (원페어) to Full House (풀하우스) is multiplied by the **completion multiplier** (4.2). It is not applied to Four of a Kind (포카드), Straight Flush (스트레이트 플러시) or Royal Straight Flush (로얄 스트레이트 플러시).
  - If it comes closer to several hands, only the largest of those values is used.
- If the deck has 5 cards and the merged card is lower than the lowest grade in the deck (when merging in storage), every kind has weight 1.
- If every kind has weight 1, all possible kinds have the same odds (4.4 below).

### 4.2 Strength (grade of the card made)

| Grade made | Strength |
|---|---|
| C, B | 6 |
| A | 4 |
| S, SS | 2 |
| SSS to ZZZ | 1 |

Completion multiplier (only for a kind that completes a hand, from One Pair (원페어) to Full House (풀하우스)):

| Grade made | Completion multiplier |
|---|---|
| C, B | 3 |
| A | 2 |
| S to ZZZ | 1 |

| Two Pair (투페어) | 1 |

| Flush (플러시) | 4.5 |
| Full House (풀하우스) | 0.7 |
| Four of a Kind (포카드) | 1.2 |
| Straight Flush (스트레이트 플러시) | 4.5 |

## 5. Dice (rerolling a deck card)

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Materials | Dice (주사위). By card grade: 1 for D to B, 2 for A to SS, 4 for SSS to XX, 8 for XXX to ZZ, 16 for ZZZ |
| Grade | Unchanged (odds 100%) |
| Suit and number | One of the 52 kinds left after excluding those of the cards now equipped in the deck and the current suit and number of the card being rerolled. ## 6. Cards from items and rewards

The grade is fixed, and the suit and number are one of 52 kinds at equal odds (1 in 52 for each kind, 1.9231%).

| Source | Grade |
|---|---|
| Daily Card 1, 2, 3 | 1 card each of C, B and S |
| Step-up Card Pack | 3 cards. C on the 1st purchase, B on the 2nd, A on the 3rd, S on the 4th, SS from the 5th |
| Free draw (ad) | 1 D |
| Check-in day 5 | 1 B |
| Pass cards | A grade set by tier and season. At tiers 14, 17, 20 and 23, you pick 1 of 3 cards shown in advance |
