# Magical Dove Privacy Policy

{{상호}} (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and related laws in providing the mobile game "Magical Dove" (the "Game"), and explains in this policy how it handles users' personal information.

Effective date: 2026-11-13

## At a glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Sign-up | None. The Game does not ask for names, phone numbers or email addresses |
| Game progress | Stored only on the user's device. Not sent to the Company's servers |
| Payments | Purchase history is processed to confirm purchases and deliver items. Entrusted to RevenueCat (United States) |
| Ads | Google collects the advertising identifier and other information to provide ads |
| Provision to third parties | None |
| Contact | newmeans.dev@gmail.com |

## 1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information only for the following purposes, and if a purpose changes, it takes the measures required by law.

1. Confirming purchases of paid items, delivering items and restoring purchases
2. Cancellations, refunds, refunds of overpayments and handling related disputes
3. Detecting and preventing fraudulent payments and abnormal use
4. Providing ads and ad rewards, measuring ad performance and preventing invalid ad traffic
5. Handling user inquiries and complaints
6. Fulfilling legal obligations

## 2. Personal Information Processed

The Company does not offer sign-up or login. The items processed are all listed below.

### a. Items processed without consent

| Category | Items | Purpose | Legal basis | How collected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| In-app purchases | Anonymous user ID created by the payment tool, purchase history (product, transaction number, purchase token, purchase date and time, amount and currency, store type), app version, operating system type and version, country and language settings, IP address | Confirming purchases, delivering items, restoring purchases, handling refunds, preventing fraudulent payments | Personal Information Protection Act, Article 15(1)(4) (performance of a contract) | Collected automatically by the payment tool (RevenueCat SDK) when the app is launched or a purchase or restore is made |
| Retention of transaction records | The purchase history above, records of refunds and dispute handling | Keeping records under the E-Commerce Act | Personal Information Protection Act, Article 15(1)(2) (legal obligation); E-Commerce Act, Article 6 | Same as above |
| Customer inquiries | Email address, inquiry content, order numbers and screenshots sent by the user, device model and app version | Answering inquiries, handling refunds and complaints | Personal Information Protection Act, Article 15(1)(4) (measures taken at the data subject's request) | Sent directly by the user by email |

The Company does not receive payment method information such as card numbers. Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store.

### b. Items collected by the ad provider

The ad tool included in the Game (Google AdMob) collects the following information directly. The Company does not combine this information with other information that can identify users. Details are in "11. Collection of Behavioral Information by Ad Providers".

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, iOS IDFA), app set ID | Providing ads, personalized ads, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention |
| IP address (may be used to estimate an approximate location) | Same as above |
| In-app activity (app launches, ad taps, ad views) | Same as above |
| Diagnostics (app launch time, freeze rate, power use and so on) | Ad quality control |

### c. Information stored only on the device and not collected by the Company

Game progress (stages, currency, cards and items, missions and check-in records), settings (sound, vibration, notifications) and records of purchases already delivered are stored only on the user's device and are not sent to the Company or third parties. Notifications are scheduled and shown within the device, and the Company does not collect device tokens for sending notifications.

If the device operating system's backup feature (Android Auto Backup, iCloud Backup and so on) is turned on, this information may be backed up to the user's own cloud account. This is a feature of the operating system, and the Company does not access these backups.

## 3. Personal Information of Children under 14

The Game is not mainly intended for children under 14, and the Company does not collect children's personal information in ways that require a legal guardian's consent. If the Company learns that personal information of a child under 14 has been processed, it destroys it without delay. Legal guardians may request access to, deletion of, or suspension of processing of a child's personal information as described in "12. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them".

## 4. Processing and Retention Periods

| Item | Retention period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Records of contracts or cancellations (purchase history) | 5 years | E-Commerce Act, Article 6; Enforcement Decree of the same Act, Article 6 |
| Records of payments and supply of goods | 5 years | Same as above |
| Records of consumer complaints or dispute handling | 3 years | Same as above |
| Customer inquiries (not covered by the records above) | 1 year from the day the reply is completed | Checking inquiry history |
| Information collected by the ad provider | Not kept by the Company. Follows Google's policies | |

## 5. Procedure and Method of Destruction

1. Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay. If it must be kept under other laws, it is moved and stored separately and used only for that purpose.
2. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.
3. Purchase history kept in the payment tool is deleted when its retention period ends, at the Company's request to the processor.
4. Game progress stored on the device is deleted when the user deletes the app or clears its data.

## 6. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. The exceptions are where a law specifically provides for it, or where an investigative agency requests it for an investigation in accordance with the procedures and methods set by law.

## 7. Entrustment of Processing

The Company entrusts the processing of personal information as below, and through the entrustment agreement manages and supervises the processor so that it handles personal information safely.

| Processor | Entrusted work | Retention period |

## 9. Measures to Keep Personal Information Secure

The Company takes the following measures to keep personal information secure.

1. Administrative measures: keeping the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum, and managing and supervising processors.
2. Technical measures: encrypting (TLS) the connections over which personal information is sent, using 2-step verification for the payment management tool and the store console accounts, and giving access rights only to those who need them.
3. Physical measures: controlling access to devices and documents that contain personal information.

## 10. Installation, Operation and Refusal of Automatic Collection Tools

The Game does not use cookies. The device's advertising identifier may be collected automatically to provide ads, and users can block or change it as follows.

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| Android | Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID |
| iPhone | Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or turn off tracking for the Game in the list |

The Game can be used even if the advertising identifier is blocked. Ads may still appear, but they are general ads rather than ads tailored to interests.

## 11. Collection of Behavioral Information by Ad Providers

To provide ads, the Company allows the ad provider below to collect behavioral information in the Game.

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Collection tool | Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) |
| Collecting business | Google LLC (United States) |
| Items collected | Advertising identifier, app set ID, IP address, in-app activity (app launches, ad taps, ad views), diagnostics |
| How collected | Collected and sent automatically when the Game is launched or an ad is viewed |
| Purpose | Providing ads, ads tailored to users' interests (personalized ads), measuring ad performance, fraud prevention |
| Retention and use period | Follows Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

How Google uses information collected from apps is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you do not want personalized ads, you can do the following.

1. Delete the advertising identifier or turn off tracking as described in "10.".
2. Turn off personalized ads in Google My Ad Center (https://myadcenter.google.com).
3. In regions where a consent screen is provided, change your choice in the Game under Settings (설정) > Ad Privacy (광고 개인정보).

The Company does not collect behavioral information to provide personalized ads to children it knows to be under 14, and does not collect sensitive behavioral information such as ideology, beliefs or health.

## 12. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

1. Users (or legal guardians, for children under 14) may at any time request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of their personal information, and withdraw consent.
2. Requests can be made at newmeans.dev@gmail.com, and the Company acts on them and reports the result within 10 days from the day it receives them.
3. Because the Game has no accounts, you must include a store order number (for example, a Google Play order number starting with GPA) or the user ID shown on the Game's settings screen so that your information can be found. The Company may check whether the person making the request is the data subject or a legitimate representative.
4. Personal information that other laws require to be kept cannot be deleted on request, and requests for access or suspension of processing may be restricted by law. In that case, the Company tells you the reason.
5. Requests made through a representative must include a power of attorney.
