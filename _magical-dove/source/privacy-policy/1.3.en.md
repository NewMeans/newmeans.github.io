# Magical Dove Privacy Policy

{{상호}} (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and related laws in providing the mobile game "Magical Dove" (the "Game"), and explains in this policy how it handles users' personal information.

## 1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information only for the following purposes, and if a purpose changes, it takes the measures required by law.

1. Confirming purchases of paid items, delivering items and restoring purchases
2. Cancellations, refunds, refunds of overpayments and handling related disputes
3. Detecting and preventing fraudulent payments and abnormal use
4. Providing ads and ad rewards, measuring ad performance and preventing invalid ad traffic
5. Analyzing Game usage statistics, finding and fixing errors, and measuring the performance of ads that promote the Game
6. Running ads that promote the Game, measuring installs and use that come from ads, and preventing fraudulent installs
7. Providing the ranking (leaderboard)
8. Handling user inquiries and complaints
9. Fulfilling legal obligations

The Company does not offer sign-up, and the Game has no accounts of its own. The ranking on Android works through the user's Google Play Games account. The items processed are all listed below.

The Company does not receive payment method information such as card numbers. Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store.

The analytics tools and the marketing measurement tool are included only in the Game for Android. Game usage records do not contain names, phone numbers or email addresses. The player ID is a random number created by the Game and is used to recognize the same user in marketing measurement and usage analytics (see "10."). When a purchase is confirmed, the payment tool (RevenueCat) passes the details of that purchase (product, transaction number, purchase date and time, amount and currency) and the anonymous user ID to the analytics tool (Google Analytics for Firebase).

The ranking is available only in the Game for Android and unlocks at stage 10. If the user is signed in to Google Play Games, the Game sends the best stage record as that account's leaderboard score, and receives the display names, records and ranks shown on the leaderboard and shows them on screen. The Company does not store display names, player IDs or ranks, and does not send them to other tools. The user's display name and record are visible on other users' leaderboards only if the user has set their game activity to public in their Google Play Games profile. The user decides this visibility in Google Play Games and can change it at any time. If the user does not sign in to Google Play Games, only the ranking cannot be used, and the Game's other features can be used as usual.

### c. Information stored only on the device and not collected by the Company

The Game's save data (stages, currency, cards and items, missions and check-in records), settings (sound, vibration, notifications) and records of purchases already delivered are stored only on the user's device, and the save data itself is not sent to the Company or third parties. On Android, however, the Game usage records in "a." (summaries used for statistics, such as the stage reached and the amounts of currency earned and spent) are sent to the analytics tool and the marketing measurement tool, and once the ranking unlocks, the best stage record is sent to Google Play Games. Notifications are scheduled and shown within the device, and the Company does not collect device tokens for sending notifications.

If the device operating system's backup feature (Android Auto Backup, iCloud Backup and so on) is turned on, this information and the player ID may be backed up to the user's own cloud account. This is a feature of the operating system, and the Company does not access these backups.

## 3. Personal Information of Children under 14

The Game is not mainly intended for children under 14, and the Company does not collect children's personal information in ways that require a legal guardian's consent. If the Company learns that personal information of a child under 14 has been processed, it destroys it without delay. Legal guardians may request access to, deletion of, or suspension of processing of a child's personal information as described in "12. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them".

## 5. Procedure and Method of Destruction

1. Personal information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay. If it must be kept under other laws, it is moved and stored separately and used only for that purpose.
2. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.
3. Purchase history kept in the payment tool is deleted when its retention period ends, at the Company's request to the processor.
4. Game usage records and error records kept in the analytics tools are set to be deleted automatically when their retention periods end.
5. Records kept in the marketing measurement tool are deleted by the processor when their retention period ends.
6. Ranking records kept in Google Play Games are deleted when the user deletes this Game's data in their Google Play Games profile.
7. Game save data stored on the device is deleted when the user deletes the app or clears its data.

## 6. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. The exceptions are where a law specifically provides for it, or where an investigative agency requests it for an investigation in accordance with the procedures and methods set by law.

## 9. Measures to Keep Personal Information Secure

The Company takes the following measures to keep personal information secure.

1. Administrative measures: keeping the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum, and managing and supervising processors.
2. Technical measures: encrypting (TLS) the connections over which personal information is sent, using 2-step verification for the payment management tool and the store console accounts, and giving access rights only to those who need them.
3. Physical measures: controlling access to devices and documents that contain personal information.

## 10. Installation, Operation and Refusal of Automatic Collection Tools

The Game does not use cookies. The device's advertising identifier may be collected automatically to provide ads and measure ad performance, and users can block or change it as follows.

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| Android | Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID |
| iPhone | Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or turn off tracking for the Game in the list |

The Game can be used even if the advertising identifier is blocked. Ads may still appear, but they are general ads rather than ads tailored to interests.

On Android, the app instance ID created by the analytics tool is collected automatically for usage analytics. This ID disappears when the app is deleted, and a new one is created when the app is installed again.

On Android, the Game creates a random number (player ID), stores it on the device together with the Game save data, and uses it to recognize the same user in marketing measurement and usage analytics. This ID disappears when the app is deleted or its data is cleared, but it comes back together with the save data when a device backup is restored.

On Android, for the ranking, the Google Play Games tool (Play Games Services SDK) checks the user's Google Play Games sign-in status when the Game is launched. If the user is not signed in, the sign-in screen opens only when the user taps the sign-in button in the Ranking (랭킹) window. This tool sends analytics data and diagnostic data to Google to keep the tool stable and improve it.
